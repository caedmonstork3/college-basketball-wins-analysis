# What Leads to Wins in College Basketball?

## Overview

This project analyzes 2026 Division I college basketball data to determine which statistics are most strongly related to winning.

The analysis examines 17 different statistics, including offensive and defensive efficiency, effective field goal percentage, turnover rate, rebounding, free throw rate, and tempo.

## Research Question

**What leads to wins in college basketball?**

## Analysis

The project uses Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and NumPy to:

- Analyze the relationship between wins and 17 basketball statistics
- Create scatterplots and trendlines
- Compare the best and worst teams in Power Conferences
- Highlight the 2026 Final Four teams
- Analyze correlations between each statistic and wins
- Rank the statistics based on their relationship with winning

## Key Findings

The statistics most strongly related to wins were Wins Above Bubble, BARTHAG, Adjusted Defensive Efficiency, Adjusted Offensive Efficiency, and Effective Field Goal Percentage Allowed.

Adjusted Tempo had very little relationship with wins, suggesting that playing faster or slower was not strongly associated with winning.
